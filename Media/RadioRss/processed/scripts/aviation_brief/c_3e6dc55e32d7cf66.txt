Maritime brief. U.S. Naval Institute News reports that U.S. Army Picks 5 to Prototype Long-range Maritime Strike Missile. Five companies were selected to deliver prototypes of a missile capable of hitting moving, long-range maritime targets to the U.S. Army, the ground service announced Tuesday. Anduril, Castelion, Lockheed Martin Missiles and Fire Control, Mach Industries and RTX were awarded through five Other Transaction Authority agreements to develop and deliver their pitches for the ground service’s Precision Strike Missile Increment 4 program. The defense contractors are expected to deliver prototypes within Fiscal Year 2027 and receive follow-on contracts depending on their performance by FY28. Increment 4 prototypes “must be operable in the Indo-Pacific Area of Operations,” according to a request Published October 1, 2026.
