Maritime brief. U.S. Naval Institute News reports that U.S. to Transfer Surveillance Aircraft to the Philippine Coast Guard. MANILA, PHILIPPINES – The U.S. will transfer three King Air turboprop planes equipped for maritime domain awareness operations to the Philippine Coast Guard, the Pentagon announced Monday. Technology Service Corp. was awarded $39 million for the procurement and integration of intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance kits onto King Air 350 Extended Range aircraft, reads the contract announcement. Two of the three aircraft will receive the ISR hardware and software modifications. Work also includes training on the surveillance suites and operation of the planes.
