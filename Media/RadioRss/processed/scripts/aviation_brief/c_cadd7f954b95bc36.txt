Aviation brief. the Honolulu Star-Advertiser reports that Boeing workers approve latest contract offer, averting strike fears. Boeing’s largest white-collar union said on Thursday its members approved a sweetened contract offer, easing fears of a strike that could have stalled work on two long-delayed jetliners and slowed the pace of aircraft deliveries.
