This story has been updated to correct the spelling of USS Klakring (FFG-42). The U.S. Navy and Royal Navy sunk decommissioned frigate USS Klakring (FFG-42) earlier this month as part of a sinking exercise with Royal Navy forces. Newly commissioned Virginia-class submarine USS Massachusetts (SSN-798) dealt the final blow to Klakring, sending it to the bottom of the Atlantic Ocean. Massachusetts fired an Mk48 Advanced Capability Torpedo at Klakring during the Sept. 20 exercise, sinking the decommissioned frigate around 150 miles off the northwest coast of Scotland. Massachusetts was commissioned in March and was declared ready for fleet tasking in August. Published September 30, 2026.
