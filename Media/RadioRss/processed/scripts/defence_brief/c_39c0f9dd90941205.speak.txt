National security brief. U.S. Naval Institute News reports that 3 Navy Ships Decommissioned, Cruiser USS Robert Smalls Makes Final Homeport Change. Three warships were recently decommissioned as Fiscal Year 2026 came to a close on Wednesday. The Navy bid farewell to Whidbey Island-class dock landing ship USS Germantown (LSD-42 and Ticonderoga-class guided missile cruisers USS Lake Erie (CG-70) and USS Shiloh (CG-67). The 40-year-old Germantown decommissioned on Sept. 24 during a ceremony held in San Diego. Germantown was the first dock landing ship to conduct Landing Craft Air Cushion amphibious operations and served during Desert Shield, Desert Storm, Operation Iraqi Freedom and Operation Enduring Freedom. Germantown also carried out humanitarian and disaster relief missions following the 1991 eruption of Mount Pinatubo Published October 1, 2026.
