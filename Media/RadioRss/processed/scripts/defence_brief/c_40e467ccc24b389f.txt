National security brief. the U.K. Ministry of Defence reports that Graves of five soldiers killed in Normandy identified. Five missing soldiers of World War 2 who were killed during the Battle of Normandy now have named graves after Rededication ceremonies were held on 29 and 30 September 26, and 1 October 26.
