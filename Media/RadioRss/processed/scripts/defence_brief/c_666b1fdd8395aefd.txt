National security brief. the U.K. Ministry of Defence reports that Six Scottish soldiers buried in France 108 years after their deaths. MOD War Detectives used DNA evidence to identify two of the fallen, four remain unknown though one was identified as having served with the Argyll & Sutherland Highlanders whilst the other three likely served with the Gordon Highlanders. Published October 2, 2026.
