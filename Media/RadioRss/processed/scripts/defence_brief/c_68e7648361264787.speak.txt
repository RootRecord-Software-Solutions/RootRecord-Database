National security brief. U.S. Naval Institute News reports that Navy Brings In 45,112 Recruits in FY 2026. The Navy brought in at least 45,112 active-duty enlisted sailors in Fiscal Year 2026, according to the sea service’s final numbers, released Thursday. The Navy announced in July that it met its recruiting goal of 45,000, three months ahead of schedule, USNI News previously reported. It exceeded the goal by 112 sailors. The sea service will now aim to bring in 45,000 more sailors in Fiscal Year 2027, which began Thursday, according to a Navy release. The new goal is 9,000 more than the Navy budget’s books, released in April, listed. The Navy projects an end strength of 356,600 for Published October 1, 2026.
