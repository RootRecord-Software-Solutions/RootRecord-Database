Developer brief. Cloudflare reports that Announcing Cloudflare K2: serverless event streams. Cloudflare K2 is a serverless event streaming service built directly on top of R2 object storage for high-scale data movement and long-term retention. By decoupling producers and consumers at the edge, K2 enables durable, ordered log streams without the operational overhead of traditional broker clusters. Published October 1, 2026.
