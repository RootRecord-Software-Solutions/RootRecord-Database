Hawaii brief. the Honolulu Star-Advertiser reports that Man from India pleads guilty to defrauding Kaneohe kupuna. A 25-year-old man from India will be sentenced Jan. 12 after pleading guilty to his role in an international fraud scheme that conned an 80-year-old Kaneohe woman into giving him $137,458 in gold bars she bought with her life savings.
