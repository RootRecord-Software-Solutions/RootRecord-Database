Hawaii brief. the Honolulu Star-Advertiser reports that McCormick’s price hikes spice up quarterly results despite sluggish demand. McCormick reported higher quarterly sales and profit on Thursday, as pricier seasonings and sauces helped cushion the blow from weaker consumer demand due to elevated gas prices and a cyclospora outbreak that dented volumes.
