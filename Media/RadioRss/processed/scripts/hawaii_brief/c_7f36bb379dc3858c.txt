Hawaii brief. the Honolulu Star-Advertiser reports that Paramount names Mattel boss Kreiz as co-CEO. Paramount Skydance named Mattel CEO Ynon Kreiz, who led the toymaker’s transformation into a broader entertainment company, as the co-CEO of the combined firm following its $110 billion Warner Bros deal. Published October 1, 2026.
