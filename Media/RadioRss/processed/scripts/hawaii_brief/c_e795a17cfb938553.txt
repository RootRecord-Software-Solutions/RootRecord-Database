Hawaii brief. the Hawaii Department of Business, Economic Development, and Tourism reports that August 2026 Visitor Spending and Arrivals Declined. Total spending by visitors in August 2026 was $1.59 billion (measured in nominal dollars), a 9.7 percent decline compared to August 2025, according to preliminary statistics from DBEDT. Published September 30, 2026.
