SpaceX brief. Spaceflight Now reports that Launch preview: More than 100 spacecraft hitch a ride to orbit on SpaceX’s Transporter-18. The mission carried 130 payloads onboard the Falcon 9 rocket heading to a Sun-synchronous orbit. Liftoff from pad 4E at Vandenberg Space Force Base happened at 11:32 a.m. PDT (2:32 p.m. EDT / 1832 UTC). Published October 1, 2026.
