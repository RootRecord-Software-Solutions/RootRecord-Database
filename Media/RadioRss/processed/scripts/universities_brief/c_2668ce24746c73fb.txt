Universities brief. UC Berkeley reports that New UC Berkeley Journalism project will complete threatened reporters’ investigations. This first-of-its-kind U.S. lab, a partnership with Forbidden Stories, will work with a global network of journalists to keep public-interest investigations alive in the face of threats or pressure.
